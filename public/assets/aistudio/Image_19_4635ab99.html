Barista Assembly Steps
  1. 1

    Fill the standard 12 oz chilled glassware with 120g of clear artisan ice cubes.

  2. 2

    Pump 15 ml organic caramel syrup directly into the base over the ice.

  3. 3

    Pour 180 ml chilled oat milk steadily down the side to maintain clarity.

  4. 4

    Pull a fresh double espresso shot (60 ml @ 27-29s extraction) and gently float atop the milk layer.

  5. 5

    Crown with a single swirl ribbon of salted caramel drizzle. Serve with glass straw.
